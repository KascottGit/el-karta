# M11 – AI: photo detection

Status: **approved; T0 done**. Milestone branch: `m11-photo-detection`.

From PLAN.md: **pulled forward** (M11 comes before M5 and M10): from M5, `packages/plugin-power` with the device types photos can detect (outlet, switch, ceiling light, wall light) and placing, moving and deleting devices on walls and ceilings in the 2D editor; from M10, photo attachments with `region` (upload, client-side compression, linked to a room and wall). M5 and M10 then build on these instead of starting over. The eval runs on every provider kept after the provider review (Claude Haiku 5.5, and Gemini 3 Flash as the fallback, ADR 0010).

Photo pass on the M4 pipeline. User takes or uploads a photo and taps the room or wall it shows (2D or 3D). The pass gets that room's walls, openings, features and 1–3 earlier photos of the room, and returns detected features with wall-relative placement, plus suggested corrections to openings and ceiling height. Results appear in the generic suggestions UI.

**Done when:** a photo of a kitchen wall gives correctly typed outlets and switches on the right wall, roughly in the right place, that need only minor moves; eval set of 30+ photos scored for type and count accuracy.

## Design decisions for this milestone (approve with the plan)

1. **`plugin-power`, only what photos need.** `packages/plugin-power` registers four device types; M5 adds the rest (panels, breakers, connection kinds, validation). Props are Zod schemas with **optional** fields, so what nobody has seen stays unknown (principle 9):
   - `outlet`: wall; `earthed`, `outdoor`; the number of sockets in one frame is `Device.count` (PLAN.md data model); default height 0.25 m.
   - `switch`: wall; `variant` single/double/dimmer; default height 1.05 m.
   - `wall_light`: wall; default height 1.9 m.
   - `ceiling_light`: ceiling.
   - Each gets a `power-in` port; switches also `control-out` and lights `control-in` (the port kinds the synthetic generator already uses), so M5's `circuit` and `control` connections need no device migration. No connection kinds yet.
2. **Placing devices generalizes the furniture tool.** The furniture tool's "place a type picked in the panel where tapped" becomes a shared device tool in `features/devices/`, driven by the type's `placements`:
   - **wall** types attach to the nearest wall face within a fixed screen reach (like node snapping, M9b decision 2): `side` is the side the pointer is on, `offset` along the wall, `height` the type's default. Away from every wall nothing is placed.
   - **ceiling** types go in the room under the pointer (`roomAt`); outside every room nothing is placed.
   - **floor** types (furniture) behave as today.
   - A selected wall device drags along its wall and may hop to another wall face; a ceiling device drags inside its room. One `updateDevice` per drop, preview through `deviceDragStore`. Delete is the editor's existing delete command.
   - `features/power/` holds the type picker, the panel (type, label, props, height, offset) and the power markers in 2D and 3D (`features/devices/appearance.ts`).
3. **Photos are attachments, not floor data.** A photo is an `attachments` row (`kind: 'photo'`, `floor_id` set) whose `region` holds `AttachmentRegion` (core Zod schema): `{ roomId, wallId? }`, the room it was taken in and the wall it faces (no wall: the room in general, or the ceiling). The floor document does not change, so there is no schema version bump. Devices made from a photo get `attachmentIds: [photoId]` as their evidence. No migration is needed: the table, `kind`, `region`, RLS and the storage policies exist since M2.
   - `SupabaseRepository` gets `listAttachments(floorId, kind)`, `deleteAttachment(id)` (storage object and row) and `region` on `uploadAttachment`.
   - Compression in the browser with no new dependency: `createImageBitmap` (honours EXIF orientation), long side at most 2048 px, JPEG quality 0.8. Re-encoding drops EXIF, including GPS position.
   - Deleting a photo asks first, and removes its id from devices on the floor in one edit.
4. **Choosing what a photo shows uses the shared selection.** The photo panel targets whatever room or wall is selected in 2D or 3D (M9 shared selection). A wall bordering two rooms offers both as choices (core `roomsBesideWall`); the device side follows from the room. With nothing selected, the panel asks the person to tap a room or wall. "Ta foto" opens the camera on phones (`capture="environment"`), "Välj bild" picks a file. Analysis starts as soon as the upload finishes, and can be re-run.
5. **The pass reads image positions; code turns them into metres (principle 7).** The prompt lists the room's walls with letters (A, B, …, clockwise, length in metres) and the openings on each (kind, offset, width, height, sill), the existing devices (type, wall, offset, height) and the target wall. The model returns, in image coordinates normalised to 0–1000:
   - **references:** image points of known things on the target wall: wall ends at floor and ceiling level, and the corners of the listed openings, each tagged with what it is;
   - **features:** type, props it can see (gangs, switch variant), the wall letter or `ceiling`, a bounding box, a confidence, and the id of an existing device when it is one already in the model.

   Code in `packages/ai-passes/src/photo/` then:
   - fits a **homography** from the image to the wall plane (metres along the wall × metres above the floor) from the references whose plane coordinates are known (floor-level wall ends, opening corners): least squares over 4 or more points;
   - maps the centre of each wall feature's box to `offset` and `height`, clamped to the wall;
   - with fewer than 4 usable references, falls back to interpolating `offset` between the nearest references along the wall and the type's default height; such suggestions get the warning `photo.position-rough` and a lower confidence;
   - places ceiling lights at the room's centroid (several: spread along the room's long axis), always with `photo.position-rough`; M13's rules do better later;
   - with 5 or more references, checks the fit: an opening whose measured width or height differs from the model's by more than 10 cm becomes an **opening correction**, and a visible ceiling line gives a **ceiling-height** suggestion when it differs from the room's by more than 5 cm;
   - **de-duplicates**: a feature the model matched to an existing device, or of the same type on the same wall within 0.3 m of an existing device or a pending device suggestion, gives no suggestion.

6. **Suggestion kinds.** Core gets two generic built-in kinds: `device` (a `Device` without provenance; accept adds it, or replaces it when the id exists) and `room-ceiling-height` (`{ roomId, ceilingHeight }`). Opening corrections reuse the `opening` kind with the existing opening's id (accept replaces it). `features/suggestions/views.ts` gets a device ghost (the type's marker, ghosted) and shows corrections as a change ("Fönster: bredd 1,00 → 1,20 m").
7. **Context, not everything.** The client builds the pass input: the room polygon, its walls and their openings, the room's devices, and 1–3 earlier photos of the room (photos of the same wall first, then the most recent), downscaled to 1024 px to fit the 4 MB request cap. Pass id `photo`; default model Haiku 5.5 with Gemini 3 Flash as fallback (`config.ts`, ADR 0010). ADR 0011 records decisions 3, 5 and 7.
8. **Homography with `ml-matrix` (new dependency, runtime, `packages/ai-passes`).** The least-squares fit (direct linear transform) needs an SVD; `ml-matrix` is pure ESM JavaScript, widely used, and runs in the browser, Node and Deno. A hand-written 8×8 solve would only cover exactly four points.
9. **Evals (`evals/photo/`), on photos of our own home.** The repo is public, so photos stay in a git-ignored `photos/` folder, as plans do in `floorplans/`.
   - Per home, the floor as JSON (a dev-only "Exportera våning" action in the editor) so the eval has the real walls and openings.
   - Per photo, a facit entry: file, room, wall, and the expected devices (type, gangs/variant if visible, wall; optionally offset and height measured on site).
   - The orchestrator drafts the facit by looking at each photo; the person checks it (as in M4).
   - Scores: type accuracy and count accuracy per type (greedy match on type and wall), wall accuracy, and offset and height error where the facit has them. Rows go to `evals/results/photo.csv`; `pnpm eval summary` covers both passes.
   - `pnpm eval photo [--provider] [--model]`, run on Haiku and on Gemini 3 Flash.

### T0 notes (what implementers build on)

- **Detectable types come from plugins.** `ai-passes` may not import plugins, so core gained `DomainPlugin.aiHints.photo` (`PhotoHint`) and `photoDetectables(registry)`, which gives plain data per type (domain, type, description, `wall`/`ceiling` placement, default height, visible props, `countable`, default ports). The app sends that list as `PhotoInput.detectable`; the pass builds device payloads from it. `plugin-power` provides the hints.
- **The pass contract is in `packages/ai-passes/src/photo/schema.ts`** (`PhotoInput`, `PhotoOutput`). Walls are letters in `walls` order, openings `O1…` and existing devices `E1…` in array order; device types are `deviceTypeKey` (`<domain>.<type>`); references are wall or opening corners as seen in the photo (`top_left` …); output fields are `nullable`, not optional (structured output). The pass itself (prompt, validate, `toSuggestions`) is written and registered in `allPasses` by T3; T0 only added the schemas and the `photo` entry in `defaultModels`.
- `NORM`, `NormPoint`, `NormBox` and `ImageInputSchema` moved from `floorplan/` to `packages/ai-passes/src/image.ts` (second use).
- `roomAt` moved from `features/furnishing/` to core `geometry/rooms.ts`, next to `roomsBesideWall`. `roomsBesideWall` probes just off each wall face along the wall against the room polygons (rooms are stored polygons, and a corridor wall can border several rooms on one side), rather than using `wallFaces`.
- Canvas helpers shared by the floor-plan import and photos: `apps/web/src/lib/image-encoding.ts` (`encodeRegion`, `blobToBase64`, `scaleToFit`, `firstFitting`).
- `ml-matrix` 6.15.1 is a dependency of `ai-passes` and in the Deno import map.

## Known limits (so far)

- **Opening and ceiling corrections are conservative (T3).** They are only offered when the error stands out from the noise of the model's corner points (ADR 0011, "Corrections"). On the synthetic camera at 1 % noise: no false correction in 50 seeds; a 20 cm window error is found in 48/50 and a 20 cm ceiling error in 35/50 on a wall showing three openings; a 20 cm door error in only 13–26/50. A wall showing only two openings and its floor corners is ambiguous and gets no opening or ceiling corrections at all. Missing a correction was preferred over suggesting a wrong one (principles 8 and 9); the T6 evals show how this plays out on real photos.
- **T4 must pass** `hinge` and `swing` in `PhotoInput.openings` and fill `PhotoSuggestOptions.pendingCorrections` from the floor's pending `opening` and `room-ceiling-height` suggestions.
