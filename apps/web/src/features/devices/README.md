# Devices

Generic drawing of devices, shared by the 2D editor and the 3D view (ADR 0008). Domains (furniture, power) add their own tools and panels on top of this instead of a second device layer; placing and moving devices is shared here.

- `appearance.ts`: marker shape and colour per `domain:type`, with a domain and a generic fallback. `hasOwnMarker` leaves out devices in a container (breakers in a panel).
- `placement.ts`: where a device is drawn. `drawnPosition` is the resolved position, or the approximate one while the device is flagged, or none, and then the device is not drawn. `sizedFootprint` is a floor placement with a positive `size` (furniture), drawn as its footprint in 2D and a box in 3D; `size` and `rotation` follow ADR 0008.
- `device-marks.ts`: the drawn devices of a floor, cached so that unrelated edits keep the same objects.
- `device-issues.ts`: the validator's issues per device. Flagged devices take the colour of their worst issue.
- `extension.ts`: the `devices` editor extension. Its layer content sits on the `openings` logical layer above the openings, so it is in the model's Konva layer and not redrawn on every pointer move. One shape paints all markers from cached sprites; each marker has a hit-only shape (`hitFunc`, no `sceneFunc`) so it can be tapped.
- `device-shapes.tsx`: the marker sprites painter and the footprint shape, shared by the layer and the drag preview.
- `device-drag.ts`, `dragged-device.tsx`: a device being dragged (moved, turned) is put in `deviceDragStore` as its preview; the devices layer hides it and `DraggedDevice` draws the preview on the overlays, so a drag redraws only them. Handles set and clear it (`deviceMove` here, furniture's in `features/furnishing/`). `DeviceGhost` draws a device that is not where the model has it; `DevicePreviewGhost` draws a device tool's hover preview, faded.
- `new-device.ts`: `newDevice(type, id, site)` makes a manually placed device of any registered type (`DeviceTypeRef`: domain and `DeviceTypeDef`), with the type's `defaultPorts` under fresh ids (`portsFrom`), no props and no `count`, since nobody has said what they are. `storeDevice` (`device-edits.ts`) stores a changed device as one `updateDevice`.
- `device-site.ts`: where a device goes (`DeviceSite`: placement and room), pure and unit-tested.
  - `newDeviceSite` tries the type's `placements` in order. **Wall:** the nearest wall whose face is within the reach (`nearestWall`), on the side the pointer is on, at the point along the wall nearest to it, at the type's `defaultHeight`; away from every wall, nothing. **Ceiling:** the room under the pointer (core `roomAt`); outside every room, nothing. **Floor:** where tapped, in the room there if any.
  - A wall device's `roomId` is the room in front of its face (looked up 10 cm beyond it), so a device on a wall between two rooms belongs to the room it faces; a ceiling device's is its ceiling's room.
  - `movedDevice`: a wall device follows the pointer to the nearest wall face within reach, which may be another wall or the other face (it keeps its height); away from every wall it slides along its own wall. A ceiling device stays inside its room and stops at its outline (`insideRoom`). A floor device goes where it is dragged. A moved device takes the room it then faces or stands in, or keeps its old one, since `updateDevice` cannot clear an optional field.
- `device-tool.ts`: `deviceTool(spec)` makes a domain's placing tool. The domain gives the chosen type (from its side panel), where to keep the hover preview and, optionally, its own constructor (furniture's default size) and a "nothing placed" flag for a notice. The reach is the snap reach, `SNAP_PX` converted with `ctx.pxToWorld`, so it is the same number of screen pixels at every zoom and larger for a finger. A tap on a device selects it instead; a placed device is selected.
- `device-move.ts`, `device-move-handles.tsx`: a selected wall or ceiling device gets a move handle on its marker (`deviceMove`, on the overlays, which still selects on tap). The drag previews through `deviceDragStore` and the drop is one `updateDevice`; a drag that ends where it started makes no edit. Delete is the editor's delete command.
- `device-type-select.tsx`: a labelled choice among a domain's types by their Swedish names, shared by the domains' panels.
- `type-name.ts`: a type's Swedish name is the i18n key `devices.types.<domain>.<type>`, falling back to `devices.types.generic`. A domain adds its names there.
