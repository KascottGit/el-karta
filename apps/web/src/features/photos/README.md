# Photos

Photos of rooms and walls (M11 decisions 3 and 4). A photo is an `attachments` row with `kind: 'photo'` and `floor_id` set, whose `region` is core's `AttachmentRegion` (`{ roomId, wallId? }`); the floor document does not change. Each photo is analysed by the photo pass into pending suggestions (M11 T4).

- `photo-tool.ts`: the "Foton" tool shows the panel. A tap in 2D selects the wall or room hit, or else the room under the pointer (a tap on a device or opening picks its room). The selection is the editor's shared one, so a wall or room tapped in the 3D view (through `view3d/select.ts`) is the target too; a device or opening tapped in 3D is not, and the panel keeps asking for a room or wall.
- `target.ts`: what the panel adds photos of. Exactly one selected room, or one selected wall; anything else asks the person to tap a room or wall. A wall's photo belongs to a room beside it (`roomsBesideWall`): with two or more the panel offers a choice, defaulting to the first; a wall in no room takes no photos. Devices found in a photo later take their side from that room.
- `compress.ts`: decoded with `createImageBitmap(file, { imageOrientation: 'from-image' })`, so EXIF orientation is applied, scaled to at most 2048 px on the long side and re-encoded as JPEG at quality 0.8 with the shared `encodeRegion`. Re-encoding drops all EXIF data, GPS position included.
- `photos-api.ts`: the floor's photos through React Query (`photosKey(floorId)`), upload and delete over the repository wrappers in `lib/attachments.ts`, and signed URLs through its shared `attachmentUrls` cache.
- `groups.ts`: the panel lists the target room's photos by the wall they face: the selected wall first (shown even before its first photo), then the other walls, then the room in general.
- `context.ts`: the pass input without images (M11 decision 7). `roomWalls` lists the walls bordering the room (core `roomsBesideWall`, probing only walls near the room's bounds, so it does not scale with the floor) with the face into the room, clockwise on the plan around the room's centroid and **starting at the photographed wall**, so that wall is always wall A. `photoContext` adds the room (its own ceiling height, else the floor's), the openings in those walls with `hinge` and `swing`, and the devices in the room of a type photos can show (`photoDetectables(registry)`). `earlierPhotos` picks up to three other photos of the room, of the same wall first, then the most recent. `pendingOnFloor` turns the floor's pending suggestions into the pass's `pending` devices and `pendingCorrections`.
- `request.ts`: `photoRunInput` fits the request under `AI_PASS_MAX_BODY_BYTES`: the photo as stored (2048 px) or smaller, then each earlier photo at 1024 px or smaller in what is left; an earlier photo that does not fit is left out.
- `analysis.ts`: `analysePhoto` builds the input, runs the `photo` pass through the shared `ai-pass` backend (`lib/ai-pass.ts`, as the floor-plan import does), turns the output into suggestions with the pass's `toSuggestions` against the floor's pending suggestions listed just before, and commits them with `source` `ai:photo:<model>` and the run id, then invalidates `['suggestions']`. Issues left after an `invalid` run go on every suggestion. Devices carry the photo in `attachmentIds`, so accepting one records its evidence. Nothing found stores nothing. Errors end with a code, shown as `photos.analysis.errors.<code>`.
- `analyse.ts`: starts an analysis right after an upload (with the compressed file, so nothing is downloaded) and from the viewer's "Analysera igen" or the panel's "Försök igen" (the file is fetched through its signed URL). Analyses run one at a time, so a second photo of the same wall sees the first one's suggestions as pending and suggests nothing twice. Progress (`preparing`, `reading`, `saving`), the count found and errors are in `photosStore.analysis` and shown in the panel, with "Visa förslagen" opening the review. Services are replaceable for tests (`setAnalysisServices`).
- `detach.ts`: deleting a photo (after a confirm) removes its id from `attachmentIds` of the floor's devices in one undoable edit, dropping the field when it becomes empty. The photo itself cannot be undeleted.

UI text is under `photos.*`.

The e2e test (`e2e/photos.spec.ts`) needs the `ai-pass` function served with `e2e/fixtures/ai-import.env`, whose fake reply includes `e2e/fixtures/ai-photo-reply.json`: two outlets and a switch on wall A, placed roughly between its floor corners at the photo's edges.
